About CICA

An association that runs
creator work as a real industry

The Chinese Influencer and Creator Association (CICA) was formally registered on 1 November 2020 as a legally established, non-profit, national-level civil association. Our position is simple: being a creator is an industry, not a type of behaviour.

  • ◆Take the new working patterns of the individual economy seriously, and grow more professional, original content in Taiwan.
  • ◆Start from education and training: develop promising newcomers who can produce content with real value, real substance and real wit.
  • ◆Build a “Taiwan's Got Talent” style internet platform, so individuals and groups without a stage can finally step into the light.
  • ◆Bridge independent streamers with companies and investors, so the whole surrounding industry can grow together.
  • ◆Pay attention to what streamers actually need, and help underserved streamers create work opportunities.
  • ◆Our founding members span media, entertainment, education, e-commerce, law and finance.

Why Join

Why join CICA

Five concrete, checkable reasons — every one of them backed by an event, a news report and photographs.

  • 01

    A stage backed by the city government and a media group

    CICA works alongside the Taipei KOL Festival and the TY Star Taipei Influencer Awards, sharing the floor with the annual event driven by the Department of Commerce of the Taipei City Government, the Taiwan New Media Development Association and the Want Want China Times Media Group. The physical stage on Xinyi Shanti Avenue and the lenses of a hundred media outlets are your exposure slot.

  • 02

    A real network of a hundred influencers and KOLs

    Since registering in 2020, the association has brought together more than a hundred influencers and KOLs every year. This is not an online exchange of likes; it is a circle where people actually meet at award ceremonies, carnivals, talks and dinners, and lift each other up.

  • 03

    Access to brand, business district and government projects

    CICA has long-standing cooperation with Taipei's business district organisations, with brands and with city government resources, including recommendation through the TOPS Taipei shopping promotion platform. When a brand goes looking for creators, the association is that shortlist.

  • 04

    Cross-border opportunity: Vietnam, Southeast Asia and the halal market

    The association has led delegations to the Taiwan–Vietnam B2B investment promotion forum, travelled to Phu Quoc to sign a memorandum of understanding with HALAL MART VIETNAM, and launched the Taiwan Brand Overseas Showcase programme. Your content skills can plug straight into overseas distribution.

  • 05

    From newcomer to professional: education, training and recognition

    We start from education and training, developing promising newcomers, and we keep speaking up for the idea that creator work is an industry. We also support underserved streamers and nurture creators with disabilities and senior creators, so the stage does not belong to only a few.

TY Star Award

TY Star Taipei Influencer Awards
so what exactly is it?

Plenty of creators pause the first time they see the name “TY Star”. Here is the whole story, once and properly.

TY

Everyone knows YT means YouTube. So what about TY?

TY is the English identifier the Taipei KOL Festival has always used, appearing in the event name, the official mailbox and every key visual. The organisers have never published an official full form for those two letters, so we are not going to invent a story for it either.

Star is the straightforward half: the awards are divided by follower count into Star, Moon and Sun tiers, with further categories for e-commerce sales, bloggers and the business district sustainability star. Put together, the official full name is “TY STAR Taipei Influencer Awards”.

A note on who runs it: The TY Star Taipei Influencer Awards are jointly organised by the Department of Commerce of the Taipei City Government, the Taiwan New Media Development Association and the Want Want China Times Media Group (executed by Sun Star Internet Technology). CICA is a partner organisation that helps promote the awards, and Chairwoman Cheng Li-Si has repeatedly represented the association in the media to explain the judging format and what it means for the industry.

Six categories, each with its own entry line

  • ⭐

    Star tier

    Up to 20,000 followers — a way in for creators who are only getting started.

  • 🌙

    Moon tier

    20,000 to 200,000 followers — a settled community, right on the edge of the next step.

  • ☀️

    Sun tier

    Over 200,000 followers — the year's honour for top-tier creators.

  • 🛒

    E-commerce tier

    Also known as the selling tier, judged on real sales performance; 7 awards were given in 2024.

  • ✍️

    Blogger tier

    Added for the first time in 2024, won on the strength of written content.

  • 🌱

    Business district sustainability star

    Launched in 2025, linked to the national carbon collection platform to drive creator sustainability missions.

How judging works: first round → second round → public vote → grand final (professional jury plus online votes). Nearly 300 influencers entered the third edition in 2023; the fifth edition in 2024 awarded 9 Sun, 8 Moon, 10 Star, 7 e-commerce and 1 blogger prize; the 2025 sustainability category drew 30 entries with cash prizes for the top three; the seventh edition in 2026 opens submissions worldwide, with no nationality or age limit.

Milestones

Our track record

From registration and industry recognition to the city government stage, the Golden Zhuo Awards and cross-border moves into Vietnam and the halal market — thirteen chapters, all documented in the press.

  1. 2020Nov

    The Chinese Influencer and Creator Association is formally registered

    CICA completes registration as a national-level civil association, with Cheng Li-Si as founding Chairwoman. The founding members come from media, entertainment, education, e-commerce, law and finance, united by the belief that the new working patterns of the individual economy deserve to be taken seriously.

  2. 2021Jun

    Making the case that creator work is an industry

    In interviews with Yam News and BaoNews, the Chairwoman argues publicly that being a creator is an industry rather than a type of behaviour, and explains why the association was founded: to start from education and training and develop promising newcomers.

  3. 2022Dec

    2022 Taipei KOL Festival results showcase and TY Star ceremony

    The annual event driven together with the Taipei Department of Commerce, the Want Want China Times Media Group and the Taiwan New Media Development Association brought creators together and helped Taipei's business districts connect with the world.

  4. 2023Nov

    2023 Taipei Influencer Carnival ‧ Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an attends

    Held on Xinyi Shanti Avenue, where Mayor Chiang Wan-an said that “anyone brave enough to express their own view can become an influencer”. The Chairwoman attended with a hundred of the association's leading creators and publicly committed to nurturing creators with disabilities and senior creators. Partner business districts included the Wanhua street district, the Maokong leisure industry, the Aiguo East Road international bridal street and Taipei leisure agriculture.

  5. 2024Oct

    Tamkang University talk on innovative livestream service models and cross-industry exchange

    Delivered at Tamkang University's Taipei campus, with the Chairwoman speaking on TikTok and Douyin livestream operations and sharing front-line experience from advising million-follower creators across the Strait.

  6. 2024Nov

    2024 Taipei KOL Festival ‧ 5th TY Star Taipei Influencer Awards

    The awards ceremony and influencer carnival took place on Xinyi Shanti Avenue, with popular creators and entertainers including 柯以柔 (Ko Yi-jou), 蹦闆, 蕭彤雯 (Hsiao Tung-wen), 那對夫妻 (That Couple), 詹姆士 (Chef James), 南珉貞, 安芝儇, 羅嵐 and 愛愛LOVE on stage.

  7. 2024

    8th Golden Zhuo Awards ‧ CICA serves as co-organiser

    The association acted as co-organiser and Chairwoman Cheng Li-Si hosted the ceremony, carrying the energy of the creator industry onto a cross-disciplinary awards stage.

  8. 2025Sep

    Delegation to Hanoi ‧ Taiwan–Vietnam B2B investment promotion and trade matching forum

    The association led a Taiwanese business delegation to meet around 900 representatives from 34 Vietnamese provinces and cities, ministries, industry associations and overseas Vietnamese communities, covering healthcare, biotech, wellness, technology, manufacturing, green energy, agriculture and education.

  9. 2025Nov

    Partnering the 2025 Taipei KOL Festival ‧ Deputy Mayor Lin Yi-hua opens the event

    Held on Shanti Avenue in the Xinyi district, gathering more than a hundred influencers and KOLs. The TY Star awards were presented at the same event, linked to the national carbon collection platform to drive creator sustainability missions.

  10. 2026Jul

    Golden Zhuo Awards 10th anniversary ‧ co-organiser, host and judge

    CICA served as co-organiser, with Cheng Li-Si acting as co-organiser representative, master of ceremonies and judge. The edition produced 36 winners, with veteran artist 黃香蓮 (Huang Hsiang-lien) receiving the Lifetime Achievement Award in Arts and Culture.

  11. 2026Aug

    MOU signed with HALAL MART VIETNAM on Phu Quoc

    The association led a Taiwanese delegation to visit local halal supermarkets, suppliers and partners, using Phu Quoc as a base and moving early on the commercial opportunities around the 2027 APEC summit.

  12. Ongoing

    Taiwan Brand Overseas Showcase ‧ two months of free physical display

    Working with HALAL MART in Vietnam to help Taiwanese brands enter Vietnam and Southeast Asia. Categories open for applications: food, health supplements, beauty and cosmetics, household goods, pet food and supplies, cultural and creative products, and Taiwan speciality goods; brands without halal certification may also apply.

  13. Ongoing

    Working with Taipei City Government resources ‧ recommendation via the TOPS platform

    Connecting city government channel resources so that the brands and products of our partners get seen by more people.
